<!-- compiled_truth -->
Across all topics, Chat proactively searches when facts essential to the answer are uncertain, potentially outdated or unsupported. Stable certain knowledge and transformations of supplied material need no unnecessary retrieval. Deliver the requested answer first when supported, with concise evidence and sources; avoid boilerplate and teaching the user how to find an answer instead of doing available verification. Prior assistant text is context, not verified evidence. Never invent certainty. Preserve original read-only tools, existing search/read allowances and permissions; introduce no keyword interception. Prompt compliance and real-provider quality require behavioral verification beyond composition tests.
